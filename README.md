### Project Description
The Procrastination Timer is a web application designed to track and celebrate your total avoidance of productive work. It turns unproductivity into a dashboard experience complete with badged achievements, custom alarm reactions, and an active idle streak tracker.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
People are spending too much time being productive without any structured metrics to measure how effectively they are avoiding their actual responsibilities.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
A dedicated, high-tech command center that logs every minute spent staring at walls or spiraling down Wikipedia rabbit holes, rewarding your complete lack of progress with badges worth absolutely nothing.
